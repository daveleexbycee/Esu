About & Philosophy

The heart of the Circle

We are everyday people who decided that help should not be hard to find.

What is a Giving Circle?

A giving Circle is a group of people with shared values that get together to create change. They decide on a cause that they care about.

This is a giving network. You will meet good people who can help you grow in business, career, and life. If you ever need help or support, you don’t suffer alone. You speak up in the circle and the circle stands with you. That’s our promise.

In this Circle, when you have, you give. When you need, you receive. No shame. No begging. Just Community.

We are everyday people — traders, public workers, creatives, and organizations — who decided that help should not be hard to find.

Our Promise of Mutual Support

If you ever need help or support, you don’t suffer alone. You speak up in the circle and the circle stands with you. That’s our promise.

You will meet good people who can help you grow in business, career, and life. The Circle is a living network of trust — not a formal charity with applications and waiting lists, but a community of neighbors, friends, and fellow travelers who choose to look out for one another.

When you have

You give — time, skill, resources, introductions, or encouragement.

When you need

You receive — without shame, without begging, with dignity.

The Future of Philanthropy

There are thousands of founders local and abroad. When we shift the power of philanthropy into all of our voices, we change who gives, how we give, and ultimately what gets funded. Giving by, for, and with the community we represent is the future of philanthropy.

Reaching those who are neglected, disempowered, and don’t have a voice. Maybe you desire to help animals and livelihoods, or to aid in disaster relief and recovery. Most importantly: do you want to learn how to turn this passion into actual service that serves the public and receive the grand funding to make it happen?

Are you passionate about helping people? Do you have a dream to let people of less circumstance be the best they can be?
