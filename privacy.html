Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2025

1. Information We Collect

When you join the Circle we collect the information you provide in the sign-up form: name, phone number, email address, home address, age range, date of birth, county, and ZIP code. We may also collect information you later share within the community, including profile updates and optional payout methods (bank or mobile-money details, PayPal, etc.) that you add so the Circle can send approved support funds. Payout details are stored securely and are visible only to you and authorized Circle administrators.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Process your membership and connect you with the Circle
  • Communicate with you about Circle activities and mutual support opportunities
  • Maintain the safety and integrity of the community
  • Improve our services and understand the needs of members

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information with other Circle members as needed for mutual support, and with service providers who help us operate the community (under confidentiality agreements). We may also disclose information if required by law.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you share with us. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we encourage members to share sensitive details only as needed.

5. Your Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us. You may also leave the Circle at any time.
