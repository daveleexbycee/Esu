What We Stand For

These values shape every conversation, every gift, and every act of support inside the Circle.

Safer

A trusted space where people can ask for help and offer support without fear or judgment.

Community-First

Decisions, care, and resources flow from and for the people inside the Circle.

Honest

We speak plainly about needs and resources. Transparency builds the trust that makes mutual aid work.

Memorable

Experiences of real help leave lasting marks. We aim for relationships and impact people remember.

Unity Creatives & Community

We welcome artists, builders, organizers, and everyday helpers who want to create change together.

How a Giving Circle Works

Simple, human, and powerful.

This is a giving network. You will meet good people who can help you grow in business, career, and life. If you ever need help or support, you don’t suffer alone. You speak up in the circle and the circle stands with you. That’s our promise.

A giving Circle is a group of people with shared values that get together to create change. They decide on a cause that they care about.

Our Purpose

There are thousands of founders local and abroad. When we shift the power of philanthropy into all of our voices, we change who gives, how we give, and ultimately what gets funded. Giving by, for, and with the community we represent is the future of philanthropy.

Reaching the neglected

Reaching those who are neglected, disempowered, and don’t have a voice. Maybe you desire to help animals and livelihoods, or to aid in disaster relief and recovery.

Passion into funded service

Most importantly: do you want to learn how to turn this passion into actual service that serves the public and receive the grand funding to make it happen?

Are you passionate about helping people? Do you have a dream to let people of less circumstance be the best they can be?
